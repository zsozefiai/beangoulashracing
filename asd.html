Beangoulash Racing Team

The BMW M4 GT3 (EVO) is a track-only endurance race car based on the G82 M4 Coupe. It features a 3.0-liter twin-turbo inline-six producing up to 590 hp and 516 lb-ft of torque, paired with a rear-wheel-drive 6-speed sequential gearbox. Built with lightweight carbon-fiber bodywork around an FIA safety cage, it weighs about 1,265 kg (2,789 lbs) and includes double-wishbone suspension, air conditioning, and a detachable Fanatec steering wheel that doubles as a sim-racing controller.
